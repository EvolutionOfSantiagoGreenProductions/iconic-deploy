15 Missed Royalty Streams for Beat Producers

ASCAP, BMI, The MLC, SoundExchange, Content ID, sync licensing, neighboring rights, and more — explained plainly, with action steps for each.

  • The MLC — Most producers don't know it exists. It collects mechanical royalties for digital streams directly.
  • SoundExchange — Separate from your PRO. Covers satellite radio, internet radio, and streaming performance royalties.
  • YouTube Content ID — Monetize every upload that uses your beat, even ones you didn't upload.
  • Neighboring Rights — International royalties for master recordings, often unclaimed for years.
  • Sync Licensing — TV, film, ads, and trailers. Step-by-step to submit placements legally.
